Services

Relocations

Whether you are starting a new beginning in a place you call home or getting an upgrade in your career. We deal with the big and not-so-big possessions that you require moved. For those moving into a new home, we begin with pre-packing, packing, loading and labelling. We handle the stressful side of moving, while you take care of your small valuables carriable in the back of your car.

Packaging

Each good is assessed to provide the best material for packaging available and compliant with modern standards. It could be furniture with each piece individually wrapped in plain white paper and flat sheet or double sided cushioning craft. Perhaps specially created pack mats to protect those corners and edges susceptible to damage, or specialised wrapping and cushinoning materials for those fine art pieces and antique having a special place in your heart.

Distribution

We distribute from Gaborone or our premises in Mogoditshane to any part of the country. Rates depend on the size of the vehicle used. Additionally, we also do part deliveries of goods less or equal to a 1000g(>=1kg) within a set 25km radius from our premises in Mogoditshane

Shipping

Shipping of small household items to large heavy-duty industry equipment is done by rail, sea or air. Options include less than container load (LCL) or full container load (FCL) depending on your needs and requests. Despite Botswana being a landlocked country, be rest assured all international moves will be facilitated through our well known and recognized agents.
LCL: Cargo space is divided among several customers
FCL: The entire container is rented for use by a single individual

FAQs

Your goods are handled with care to ensure they are delivered in the same state you gave them to us.

The duration is specified during contract signing and given based on known factors. Changes in expected time of arrival will be notified to the customer to ease concerns.

Restrictions on weight are based on service purchased. We provide deliveries to all parts of Botswana and ship to selected countries through our external partners.

Charging is decided on distance between pickup and dropoff location, and weight of package.
