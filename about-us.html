About Us

Brief History

Our roots trace back to August 2008 with a young man who had a dream to travel and leave his mark on the world. This dream manifested in the form of a company that travels to all corners of Botswana and with one day to have its name on buildings across Botswana's borders traverses the vast ocean.

Today this company boasts a formidable reputation with major stakeholders and partners. It has expanded to a nationwide enterprise with centers both locally and internationally. With plans to expand to increase branches in Botswana we are on a way to taking the world by storm.
